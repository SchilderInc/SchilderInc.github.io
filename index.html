About

With over 20 years bridging business and technology in financial services, I bring a unique perspective that few in the industry share — I speak both languages fluently, and that's what sets me apart.

I'm a strong believer that banking's next chapter will be defined by interoperable cores and ecosystem-ready platforms — and that shift is happening now.

I work at the intersection of core banking, platform openness, and modern integration — helping financial institutions unlock secure data access and scalable innovation without compromising stability.

My perspective was shaped inside banks, beginning in the JPMC branch network and evolving through digital, cloud, and core modernization roles before leading open banking initiatives across banks, credit unions, fintechs, and technology providers.

Today at Fiserv, I focus on advancing open banking and platform openness within core account processing environments — with an emphasis on interoperability, API strategy, and ecosystem enablement.

The hardest work in financial services still happens at the core — and that's where I spend my time.
